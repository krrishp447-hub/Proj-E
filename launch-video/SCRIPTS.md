# Scout launch video: scripts for selection

Length is 24 s (720 frames at 30 fps). The master is 16:9, with 1:1 and 9:16 cut-downs.
Brief: show trust, safety and premium quality. Every option is built so it reads with the sound off.

---

## 1. What we're launching

**Scout: creator intelligence for brands and agencies.** You describe a campaign in one plain-English sentence, and Scout returns a ranked shortlist of the right creators, not follower count.

| Proof point (already in the product) | Why it matters for trust |
|---|---|
| Ranks on 8 signals: sub-niche fit, engagement, growth, reach, consistency, geography, platform, **proven on-platform results** | Not a popularity contest |
| Every score is **explainable**, with a per-signal breakdown | Nothing hidden, so you can forward it to a client |
| **Authenticity score + % suspicious followers** on every creator | Protects the budget from fake reach |
| **"Verified 2d ago"** freshness stamps | The data isn't stale |
| **Audience-overlap detection** in Compare | Don't pay twice for the same viewers |
| Search → Rank → Compare → Shortlist → Connect in one flow | Hours become minutes |

Taglines already in the product and poster, reused verbatim so the brand stays consistent:
*"Find the right creators, not more creators."* · *"One search. The right ten."* ·
*"Ranked on what actually worked."* · *"Verified, not inflated."* · *"From brief to shortlist in minutes."*

## 2. Target audience

**Primary: in-house brand and influencer marketers at Indian D2C and consumer brands.** Think e-commerce, FMCG and consumer tech/audio. They own the creator budget and answer for its results.
- India's influencer market is about ₹3,000–3,500 cr in 2025 and growing about 22% a year. E-commerce (23%) and FMCG (19%) spend the most. 93.1% of brands treat Instagram as their primary channel. ([Kofluence 2026 report](https://www.mediainfoline.com/brand/kofluence-launches-2026-influencer-marketing-report))
- Spend is becoming accountable. 46.4% of brands judge influencer performance campaign by campaign, and 13.3% tie it to formal revenue targets. ([Medianews4u](https://www.medianews4u.com/15-of-indias-creators-are-now-registered-business-entities-as-influencer-marketing-crosses-a-structural-threshold-kofluence/))
- The pain is fake reach and wasted budget. Industry coverage in India puts brand wastage at 30–40% and reports widespread fake followers. ([Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/guest-post/fakefluencers-draining-brand-budgets-ai-tools-fix-indias-influencer-problem-9601457), [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/about-60-instagram-influencers-in-india-have-fake-followers-report-133988.html))

**Secondary: influencer-marketing agencies** running multi-creator campaigns for clients. They need shortlists they can defend: explainable scores and overlap checks.

**Not the target here: creators.** At most one end-card line ("Free for creators").

**What this means for the video:**
- The viewer is a skeptical professional, not a consumer.
- Trust beats hype. Show the receipts (scores, verification, overlap) rather than adjectives.
- They watch on LinkedIn with the sound off, on the website hero, and on a pitch-deck screen.

## 3. What the research says (applied below)

- **Win the first 3 seconds.** Put the product name and the problem it solves on screen within the first six words. One clear point, front-loaded. ([TripleDart](https://tripledart.com/saas-ppc/best-video-ads-saas), [Advids](https://advids.co/insights/the-art-of-the-hook-capturing-attention-instantly-in-plg-saas-youtube-videos))
- **Make it work with the sound off.** Most feed video is watched muted, so every key line appears as on-screen text. ([Growthspree](https://www.growthspreeofficial.com/blogs/b2b-video-ad-scripts))
- **Use the structure hook → problem → value → proof → CTA.** Don't try to do every job in 25 seconds. ([TripleDart](https://www.tripledart.com/blog/youtube-video-ad-examples-for-saas))
- **Premium means restraint.** One hero moment and supporting motion around it. Soft-accelerate, gentle-settle easing with no bounce. Primary elements move first and secondary ones follow on a small stagger. ([MengTo animation systems](https://www.ui-skills.com/skills/mengto/animation-systems/))
- **Sound is built in escalating waves:** a quiet open, rising stakes, then a run of hits before the title card. A riser tells the viewer the important part is about two seconds away. ([Violet Recording](https://violetrecording.com/sound-design-for-trailers/), [Morphic](https://morphic.com/ai-glossary/riser))

**Motion language for all scripts:**
- **Palette:** the poster's ink `#0C0D0B`, paper `#EFEDE6` and signal blue `#4D74FF`. Emerald `#16C98D` is used only for scores and "verified". Magenta appears once at most.
- **Type:** Anton for display, Space Mono for every number.
- **Easing:** `power3.out` and `expo.inOut` only. Hold every readable line for at least 1.2 s.
- **No real brand logos on screen.** That's a trust and legal risk.

## Script A: "Signal" (recommended)

> Hundreds of creators as a noisy field of light. One sentence turns the noise into ten verified signals.

**Why it fits:**
- It's premium: one continuous 3D world and a single hero transformation.
- It shows trust: the proof layer (authenticity, verified, overlap) is the payoff, not an afterthought.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 | Black. About 600 faint points drift in a three.js field, slightly out of focus. Slow dolly in. | **"Every brand is looking for creators."** → **"Most find followers."** | Low pad, sub pulse. Soft tick per word. |
| 3.0–6.0 | A few points flicker red and dim (suspicious accounts). The field feels unstable. | **"Some of them aren't even real."** | Pad detunes slightly. Muted glitch ticks. |
| 6.0–9.5 | A search bar settles in from the field's centre. Text types: *"Indian YouTube tech creators who review earbuds, 50K–500K…"* | **SCOUT** wordmark appears top-left | Typing ticks (sparse). Riser starts at 8.0. |
| 9.5–10.5 | **Hero moment:** the field collapses. 590 points fall away and 10 lock into a column. | none | **Hit** at 9.5. Sub drop. |
| 10.5–15.0 | The 10 points become ranked rows (real Scout UI). Emerald score rings draw 95, 91, 87… The camera eases down the list. | **"One search. The right ten."** | Beat enters (90 BPM), light percussion. |
| 15.0–19.5 | Push into the top row. The proof layer unfolds: **92 authentic · 3% suspicious · verified 2d ago**, plus a short per-signal breakdown. Then a cut to the overlap bar: *Arjun × EarbudExpert 71% → swap one*. | **"Verified, not inflated."** | Each chip lands on a beat (soft hit). |
| 19.5–24.0 | Everything recedes into the field, which is now calm and ordered. **SCOUT** wordmark. | **"Find the right creators, not more creators."** + URL | Final chord resolves. Tail fades by 23.5. |

**Music brief:** 90 BPM (one bar = 2.67 s, so 9 bars = 24 s). Minimal cinematic electronic: felt piano or soft pad, sub pulse, sparse percussion entering at the hit. Major-key resolution in the last bar. References to search on licence libraries: *"minimal cinematic tech", "elegant corporate ambient", "trust/innovation underscore"*.

**Build:**
- Remotion with `@remotion/three` for the particle field, driven only by `useCurrentFrame`.
- `@remotion/gsap` timelines for the UI choreography.
- Real Scout UI components rebuilt in the video project.
- Hardest of the three to build, and the most distinctive.

## Script B: "Receipts" (typographic, trust-first)

> Follower counts can be bought. Scout ranks on what actually worked, and shows its receipts.

**Why it fits:**
- It's the most trust-forward and the safest message.
- It's Swiss and editorial, the same language as the poster site: bold type on flat colour fields. Premium comes from restraint and timing.
- It's cheap to make: no 3D, almost all typography and UI.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.5 | Ink field. A huge Space Mono counter races upward: 12,400 → 1.4M. | **"1.4M FOLLOWERS"** | Fast ticking climb. |
| 2.5–5.0 | The number freezes. A red strike line draws through it. Underneath: **34% SUSPICIOUS**. | **"Followers can be bought."** | Hard stop, silence for 0.3 s, then a low hit. |
| 5.0–8.0 | Wipe to a signal-blue field. Anton type slams in line by line, alternating sides (the poster's scrub motion). | **"RESULTS CAN'T."** | Pulse starts (100 BPM). |
| 8.0–11.0 | The SCOUT wordmark lands. A query types itself and the ranked list builds. | **"One search. Ranked on what actually worked."** | Whoosh into the UI, ticks on each row. |
| 11.0–17.5 | Three "receipts" in quick succession, each a clean UI close-up with a mono label: ① per-signal score breakdown bars grow, ② **18 campaigns · 74% repeat · 1.3× delivered**, ③ overlap warning. | **"Explainable." · "Proven." · "No double-paying."** | Each receipt lands on a downbeat. |
| 17.5–21.0 | Paper field. Shortlist card stamps **VERIFIED ✓ 2d ago** in emerald. | **"Verified, not inflated."** | Soft stamp, warm chord. |
| 21.0–24.0 | Ink. Wordmark and tagline. | **"Find the right creators, not more creators."** + URL | Button hit. Ring-out. |

**Music brief:** 100 BPM (one bar = 2.4 s, so 10 bars = 24 s). Tight, confident modern pulse: muted plucks, sub bass, clean claps from 5 s. A deliberate 0.3 s silence at 2.5 s is the moment that grabs attention. References: *"confident minimal pulse", "modern corporate percussive", "editorial fashion beat (restrained)"*.

**Build:** Remotion and GSAP only. Fastest to produce, and the easiest to cut into 9:16.

## Script C: "One sentence" (product-led, Apple-style UI film)

> One unbroken camera move through the real product, from a sentence to a shortlist.

**Why it fits:**
- It's the clearest demonstration: buyers see exactly what they get.
- It's premium through one continuous move and perfectly timed UI states, with no cuts.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–2.0 | Extreme close-up of a blinking cursor in Scout's search bar, on a soft blue-black gradient. | **"Describe who you need."** | Room tone, one soft tick. |
| 2.0–6.0 | The camera pulls back as the query types. The "Scout understood" tokens pop in: *Tech · YouTube · India · tws, earbuds · 50K–500K · strong engagement*. | (the query is the text) | Typing in rhythm. Beat enters at 4.0 (120 BPM). |
| 6.0–10.0 | Continuous tilt down. A brief skeleton shimmer, then ranked rows cascade in with drawing score rings. | **"Ranked on 8 signals. Not follower count."** | Each row on an eighth note. |
| 10.0–14.0 | The camera glides right into "Why this match": breakdown bars grow, authenticity chip, "verified 2d ago". | **"Every score explained."** | Drop to half-time feel. |
| 14.0–18.0 | Three rows get shortlisted (check animations). The compare sheet slides in and the overlap bars fill. A warning highlights 71%. | **"See overlap before you pay twice."** | Riser into 18.0. |
| 18.0–20.0 | "Send outreach to all 3". A confirmation toast. | **"Brief to shortlist in minutes."** | Hit on send. |
| 20.0–24.0 | The UI dissolves into the SCOUT wordmark. | **"Find the right creators, not more creators."** + URL | Outro chord, fade. |

**Music brief:** 120 BPM (one bar = 2.0 s, so 12 bars = 24 s), the easiest grid for cutting on the beat. Bright but controlled electronic: arpeggiated synth, light four-on-the-floor from 4 s, half-time at 10 s. References: *"modern tech product launch", "clean upbeat electronic 120 bpm"*.

**Build:** Remotion and GSAP, using a 2D/3D CSS camera over the rebuilt UI. No three.js needed. Medium effort.

## Script D: "500 tabs" (problem → calm)

> Chaos of manual research, then silence and one search box.

**Why it fits:**
- It has the strongest emotional contrast and an instantly relatable hook.
- Premium comes from the cut to stillness.
- Risk: it overlaps with the existing product film's "problem" scene, and it's less about trust.

| Time | Picture | On-screen text | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.0–4.0 | Browser tabs and profile cards multiply and stack, with a spreadsheet filling up. The tempo accelerates and the frame gets cluttered. | **"500 tabs. 6 hours. One shortlist."** | Clicks and notification pings accelerating, then a riser. |
| 4.0–5.0 | **Hard cut to silence.** Empty ink frame, one search bar. | none | Total silence (a sound-off-proof beat). |
| 5.0–12.0 | The query types, the field fills calmly, and ranked rows arrive with score rings. | **"Or one search."** → **"The right ten."** | Calm pulse fades in (95 BPM). |
| 12.0–19.0 | Trust layer: authenticity, suspicious %, verified stamp, overlap warning, each a clean beat. | **"Verified, not inflated."** | Soft hits on beats. |
| 19.0–24.0 | Wordmark end card. | **"Find the right creators, not more creators."** + URL | Resolve. |

**Music brief:** chaos sound design for 0–4 s, silence at 4–5 s, then 95 BPM ambient pulse. References: *"calm minimal corporate", "focus ambient tech"*.

**Build:** Remotion and GSAP. Medium effort.

## My recommendation

**Pick A ("Signal") as the launch hero film, and B ("Receipts") as the 9:16 social cut-down.**
- A has the single unforgettable visual (noise collapsing into ten verified signals) and lands all three brief words: premium (3D, restraint), trust (receipts) and safety (suspicious accounts filtered out).
- B reuses A's UI components, so a second deliverable is cheap.

## Before production: claims check

- On-screen numbers (95 match, 92 authentic, 18 campaigns, 74% repeat, 71% overlap) are **illustrative demo data**. Show them as product UI, never as measured customer results. Add a small *"Illustrative product data"* line on the first UI frame, or soften them.
- **"6 hrs → 4 min"** and "minutes" are product claims from the landing page. Keep them only if you can stand behind them.
- No real brand logos (boAt, Noise and others are in the demo data). Use neutral placeholders.

## Music and licensing

- **Music:** I can't compose a broadcast-quality track. Use a licensed track from a library with a commercial licence (e.g. Artlist, Epidemic Sound, Musicbed), searched with the brief for the chosen script. I'll cut the animation to its beat grid and add synced SFX (ticks, risers, hits).
- **Remotion licence:** free for individuals and companies of up to 3 people. Larger companies need a Company License. ([Remotion pricing](https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license/pricing))
